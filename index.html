Abstract

Environment structure beats algorithm choice.

Reinforcement learning is increasingly applied to sustainable energy control, yet evaluations remain fragmented across isolated tasks. We present SustainRL-Bench, a benchmark evaluating perturbation robustness, safe RL via constrained MDPs, and multi-agent RL across three energy environments — EV charging, building HVAC, and cogeneration dispatch. Across 116 configurations and 500+ training runs, the on-policy versus off-policy divide emerges as the dominant factor: PPO achieves the strongest perturbation robustness (max 23.7% degradation versus 1380% for SAC in cogeneration) and most reliable constraint tracking via PPOLag, while off-policy methods are essential for multi-agent coordination.

A greedy heuristic outperforms all RL policies in EV charging (8.65 vs PPO 6.80), challenging the necessity of RL for all energy domains. To quantify the qualitative “coupling” descriptor used in prior work, we introduce a unified coupling score κ ranging from 0.024 (EV) to 0.860 (cogeneration) that exhibits monotonic rank correlation with all three axes.

Methodology

Three Experimental Axes

Each environment is evaluated under three orthogonal lenses, totaling 116 unique configurations.

01
🔊

Perturbation Robustness

Systematic injection of state (PS), action (PA), and dynamics (PD) perturbation across nine intensity levels per channel. Reveals algorithm-specific sensitivity profiles.

  • 3 channels × 10+ levels
  • 5-seed multi-seed validation
  • Combined-perturbation tests
02
🛡

Safe RL (CMDP)

Each environment is formulated as a Constrained MDP with an environment-specific cost. Four on-policy CMDP algorithms under varying cost limits.

  • PPOLag, CPO, OnCRPO, FOCOPS
  • 4–5 cost limits per environment
  • Reward–cost orthogonality test
03
🤝

Multi-Agent RL

Physically motivated decompositions: 54 charging stations, 6 thermal zones, 4 turbines. Single vs. shared-policy MARL via Ray RLlib + PettingZoo.

  • PPO, SAC, APPO, IMPALA
  • Independent & shared policies
  • Coupling-aware analysis

Coupling Score κ

A unified inter-agent coupling score that quantifies the qualitative “loosely/tightly coupled” descriptor used in prior energy RL literature. Across our three environments, κ correlates monotonically with all three benchmark axes.

Building HVAC 0.145

Takeaways

Key Findings

  • Robustness is environment-specific. The on-policy vs. off-policy divide dominates Cogeneration; in Building, off-policy SAC is more robust than on-policy PPO — the inverse pattern.
  • Reward–cost orthogonality matters. Safe RL is only as effective as the orthogonality of the cost signal: Building (aligned, redundant), EV (partial), Cogen (orthogonal, precise).
  • Off-policy CMDP fails. SACLag fails across all three environments due to replay buffer staleness driving oscillatory Lagrange multiplier dynamics.
  • Coupling, not agent count, drives MARL. 54 stations (low coupling) > 6 zones (medium) > 4 turbines (high) for MARL feasibility — the inverse of agent count.
  • Greedy beats RL in EV Charging. A simple greedy heuristic outperforms all learned policies (8.65 vs 6.80), challenging the assumption that RL is always necessary.
